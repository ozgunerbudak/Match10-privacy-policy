Privacy Policy

Application: Match10 (com.lumorcasoftware.match10)
Developer: Lumorca Software
Effective Date: September 13, 2026

Privacy First Guarantee: Match10 is a 100% offline, single-player game. We do not collect, store, transmit, track, or share any personal data or device information. No accounts, no advertising, and no tracking libraries are used.

1. Introduction

Lumorca Software ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy details the data handling practices for the Match10 mobile application for Android.

2. Information Collection and Use

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or unique advertising identifiers.
  • No Account Required: You can play Match10 completely anonymously without registration or login credentials.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The application contains zero analytics SDKs, telemetry monitors, or behavior tracking frameworks.
  • No Advertisements: Match10 is completely ad-free. No ad networks or mediation SDKs are integrated.
  • No In-App Purchases: The app does not include payments, subscriptions, microtransactions, or digital currency.

3. Permissions

Match10 requests only the minimum device permissions necessary for core gameplay:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Exclusively used to deliver tactile haptic feedback during number matching and board clearing. You can disable vibration at any time in the in-game Settings.
  • Zero Sensitive Permissions: The app does not request access to the Internet, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Precise Location, or External Storage.

4. Local Data Storage

All game data is saved exclusively on your local device using Android Jetpack DataStore Preferences:

  • Current board layout and progress (to resume your game session).
  • Local statistics (best score, combo streak, total matches made).
  • User settings (chosen theme, audio and vibration toggles).

This data never leaves your device and is permanently removed if the application is uninstalled or cleared via Android system settings.

5. Third-Party Services

Match10 does not integrate or share data with any third-party services, APIs, or data brokers.

6. Children's Privacy

Match10 is family-friendly and safe for players of all ages, including children under 13. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR, and the Google Play Families Policy by collecting zero data from all users.

7. Security

Because Match10 operates fully offline and transmits no data over the internet, your game state is secure within Android's sandboxed storage environment.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated effective date.
