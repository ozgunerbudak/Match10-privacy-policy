# Privacy Policy for Match10

**Effective Date:** September 13, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 13, 2026  
**Developer / Publisher:** Lumorca Software  
**Application:** Match10 (`com.lumorcasoftware.match10`)

---

## 1. Introduction

Lumorca Software ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the information handled through the **Match10** mobile application for Android.

Match10 is designed as an offline, single-player number-matching puzzle game. **We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information or usage data.**

---

## 2. Information Collection and Use

- **Personal Information:** Match10 does **NOT** collect, request, or store any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, device identifiers, IP address, or location).
- **Accounts:** The app does not require any account creation, login, or registration.
- **Analytics & Tracking:** Match10 does **NOT** contain any analytics tools, telemetry frameworks, tracking libraries, or behavior-monitoring services.
- **Advertising:** Match10 is completely ad-free. It does not integrate any third-party advertising networks or SDKs.
- **In-App Purchases:** There are no in-app purchases, microtransactions, subscriptions, or financial transactions.

---

## 3. Device Permissions

Match10 requires minimal permissions to operate:
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Used exclusively to provide subtle haptic tactile feedback when tapping tiles, making valid/invalid matches, or clearing the board. Haptics can be disabled at any time in the in-game Settings.
- **No Sensitive Permissions:** The app does **NOT** request access to your internet, location, contacts, microphone, camera, photos, storage, or phone state.

---

## 4. Local Data Storage

All game data is stored strictly on your local device using Android Jetpack DataStore Preferences:
- Current active puzzle board state (for resuming games upon relaunch)
- High score, best combo, and local game statistics
- Selected theme (e.g., Midnight Paper, Clean White, Soft Pastel, Graphite, Warm Sunset)
- Audio and haptic toggle preferences

This data is stored within your device's isolated application sandbox, is never transmitted across any network, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app or clear app data via your device settings.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

Match10 does not use any third-party services, APIs, or external SDKs that collect information used to identify you.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR Compliance)

Match10 is safe for players of all ages, including children under 13:
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or any other age).
- The app complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play Families Policy.

---

## 7. Security

Because Match10 does not transmit or store your personal information on external servers, your data is never exposed to remote security risks or unauthorized third-party access. All locally stored preferences remain protected by the security architecture of the Android operating system.
